# Module Summaries (quick revision — read before the cards)

## M1 — Preliminary & Application (Rules 1–2)
The CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021 were made by the President under the **proviso to Article 309 AND clause (5) of Article 148**, after consultation with the **C&AG** for IA&AD personnel; notified as **G.S.R. 227(E) dt. 30.03.2021** (DoPPW) and in force from **publication in the Official Gazette** (Rule 1(2)) — prospectively, though they govern servants appointed **on or after 01.01.2004** (Rule 2). Eight categories are excluded under **Rule 2(a)–(h)**: Railway servants (now Railway NPS Rules 2025), casual/daily-rated, contingency-paid, AIS members (now AIS NPS Rules 2026), locally recruited staff in Indian missions abroad, contract employees, persons governed by the Constitution/other law, and persons to whom CCS (Pension) Rules apply by special/general order (e.g., pre-22.12.2003 vacancy cases per DoPPW OM dt. 03.03.2023; pre-2004 temporary-status casual labourers regularised later; mobility/technical-resignation cases per OM dt. 20.10.2023). The Rules do **not** apply suo-moto to autonomous bodies (OM dt. 25.10.2024) — EPFO adopted them expressly by its 2025 Staff Regulations with the UPSC-consultation proviso switched off. UPS (G.S.R. 599(E) dt. 02.09.2025, effective 01.04.2025) is an **option under NPS**, not a repeal.

## M2 — Definitions (Rule 3)
Rule 3(1) defines 22 terms in clauses **(a)–(v)**: Accredited Bank (incl. RBI), Accumulated Pension Corpus, Annuity, Annuity Service Provider (IRDAI-registered + PFRDA-empanelled), Authority/PFRDA (Sec. 3(1) PFRDA Act, 2013, incl. interim PFRDA), CRA (Sec. 27), NPS (Sec. 20), DDO (Gazetted), CDDO (assignment account; CPWD/Forest/CPWA Code depts), Defence Services (MoD + DAD civilians not under Army/Air Force/Navy Acts), Emoluments (= Rule 5), Foreign Service (pay not from Consolidated Fund of India/State/UT — includes domestic PSU deputation!), Government, HOD (Sch. I DFPR + President), HOO (Rule 14 DFPR Gazetted + Competent Authority), IPA, Local Fund administered by Government ("complete and direct control"), PAO (incl. AG), Pension Fund (Sec. 27(3)), PRAN, Subscriber, Trustee Bank (Banking Regulation Act, 1949). Rule 3(2): residuary definitions from FR 1922, CCS (Pension) Rules (now 2021), PFRDA Act 2013, PFRDA Regulations.

## M3 — Registration (Rule 4)
On joining, submit **CSRF + Form 1 + Form 2 immediately to the HOO** (Rule 4(1)). Chain: HOO→DDO within **3 working days of joining** (4(2)); DDO→PAO/CDDO within **3 working days of receipt** (4(3)); PAO/CDDO→CRA (online **plus signed physical copy**) within **3 working days of receipt** (4(4)). Reverse: CRA→PAO→DDO→HOO "immediately" (4(5)–(7)); HOO records PRAN in CSRF & Service Book and pastes a **certified copy** of CSRF within **5 working days** (4(8)). First contribution credited within **20 days of application OR last day of joining month, whichever is LATER** (4(9)). If PRAN is pending at first salary, salary is paid after withholding 10%, later credited **with Rule 8 PPF interest** (4(10)–(11)).

## M4 — Emoluments (Rule 5)
NPS "emoluments" = **Basic Pay (FR 9(21)(a)(i)) + NPA + DA only** — HRA, TA, special pay, deputation allowance excluded. Paid leave: actual leave salary + DA/NPA (5(2)). Absence/EOL: pay actually drawn; nil if none (5(3)). Suspension: subsistence allowance + DA on it (5(4)). Deputation **in India**: actual pay (5(5)); **foreign service (incl. domestic PSU!) or deputation outside India**: notional parent-cadre pay (5(6)). Re-employed pensioner: pay + the pension element deducted from pay (5(7)).

## M5 — Contributions, Interest & Investment (Rules 6–9)
Employee **10%**, Government **14%** (w.e.f. 01.04.2019) of emoluments, both **rounded up to the next higher rupee** (6(1), 7(1), 7(5)). During suspension the employee may opt out; if he opts out the Government also pays nil (7(3) 1st proviso); if the suspension period is finally treated as duty/paid leave, both shares are recalculated with **PPF interest** on the difference (provisos to 6(2)/7(3)). Proviso to 7(1): for leave on **medical ground / civil commotion / useful higher studies**, Government pays 14% on **notional full emoluments** even if leave salary is nil or half. Monthly calendar: DDO bill by **20th** (6(8)); PAO uploads SCF & generates Transaction ID by **25th** (6(10)(i)); remittance to Trustee Bank via Accredited Bank by **last working day** — **March contribution on the first working day of April** (proviso to 6(10)(ii)); same for Government share (7(6)). Delay compensation: Rule 8 — PPF rate, credited within **30 days** of principal credit; 2004–2012 period per DFS Notification F.No. 1/3/2016-PR dt. 31.01.2019 and DoE OM No. 1(21)/EV/2018 dt. 12.04.2019. Responsibility fixed by **HOD or CCA**; liability determined like delayed TDS under **Sec. 201(1A)** IT Act, 1961. Rule 9: PFRDA notifies pension funds/investment pattern — Default (≤15% equity, 3 PSU PFMs), Scheme G, LC25, LC50, and (from late 2025) **LC75 & BLC** for Central Govt employees under NPS & UPS.

## M6 — Rule 10 (death/disability option)
Every subscriber exercises **Form 1** (OPS/EOP vs NPS) at joining — HOO verifies and keeps it in the Service Book, copy goes HOO→DDO→PAO→CRA, PAO enters it online (10(1)–(2)). **Form 2** (family details) stays with the HOO (10(3)); changes recorded in Col. 7 — original Form 2 is never substituted during service; judicially separated spouse counts; disabled child needs a Civil Surgeon-rank medical certificate both at reporting and at claim (10(3)(c), cross-ref proviso to Rule 54(6) CCS (Pension) Rules 1972 = Rule 50(9) of 2021 Rules). Option revisable **any number of times** during service; on invalidation discharge a **fresh option** must be offered (10(4)); on death in service the **last option is final** — family cannot revise (10(5)). Defaults (10(6)): OPS/EOP if death/invalidation occurs **before 15 years' service or within 3 years of notification** (window closed 31.03.2024); otherwise **NPS** (10(6)(iii)). If the OPS option is **infructuous** (no eligible family member), it is deemed invalid and the **full NPS corpus** goes to nominee/legal heirs (10(7)).

## M7 — Rules 11–13 (superannuation, VRS, premature retirement)
Rule 11: superannuation (incl. post-extension) → PFRDA superannuation exit. Rule 12: VRS after **20 years' regular service** (ALL sanctioned leave incl. EOL & study leave counts; casual/ad-hoc/contract service does not), **3 months' written notice** to the appointing authority; not refused → deemed accepted at expiry (12(2) proviso); notice curtailable (12(3)); withdrawal only with approval, requested **≥15 days before** intended retirement (12(4) proviso); **not available** on ITEC assignments, postings abroad, or foreign-Government contract assignments until **1 year after resuming charge in India** (proviso (i)–(iii)); SVRS surplus retirees and absorption cases excluded (12(5)); VRS exit is at **superannuation level (60:40)**, not 20:80 (12(6)); IPA continuation/deferment option (12(7)). Rule 13: FR 56(j)/(k) premature/voluntary retirement and DoPT SVRS (OM No. 25013/6/2001-Estt.(A) dt. 28.02.2002) also get **superannuation-level exit** + SVRS ex-gratia (proviso to 13(1)).

## M8 — Rules 14–15 (resignation & absorption)
Ordinary resignation (14(1)): **premature exit (max 20% lump sum / min 80% annuity; 100% if corpus ≤ ₹5 lakh)**, but payout only after **90 days** from effective resignation (1st proviso); if the subscriber dies within 90 days, pay immediately (2nd proviso); may continue same PRAN as non-Government subscriber (3rd proviso). **Technical resignation** to another NPS-covered Govt dept: same PRAN continues (14(2)); to a **non-NPS** Govt dept: **superannuation-level (60:40) exit** or continue same PRAN (provisos). Withdrawal of resignation in public interest only if all five conditions of 14(3)(a)–(e) are met (not temporary; compelling reasons + material change; clean intervening conduct; absence ≤ 90 days; post available) — and **never** if resignation was to join a private company/PSU/autonomous body (14(4)); allowed withdrawal deemed to condone the break (14(5)). Rule 15: permitted absorption in PSU/AB → deemed retirement, **60:40 exit**; if NPS exists in the new body, same PRAN **must** continue (1st proviso); if not, exit or continue PRAN (2nd proviso). Date of absorption per Explanation (1)(i)–(iii).

## M9 — Rules 16–20 (invalidation, disablement, penalties, death)
Rule 16: Sec. 20 RPwD Act, 2016 protection first (disability certificate under RPwD Rules, 2017); mandatory counselling before processing an invalidation request (2nd proviso to 16(2)); application to HOD (spouse, failing which family member, may apply if he is incapacitated). Medical examination: HOO/HOD requests within **15 days**, examination within **30 days**; **Medical Board** for all Gazetted and non-Gazetted with pay **exceeding ₹54,000 p.m.** (FR 9(21)), else Civil Surgeon/DMO; lady doctor on the Board for a woman; certificate in **Form 3**; lower post if fit for less laborious service and willing (16(3)–(8)). On invalidation/disablement: if OPS/EOP option/default — IPA closed, **Government share + returns to Government account**, employee share + returns paid to subscriber/nominee **in lump sum** (16(9)–(12), 17(1)–(4), 20(2)); if NPS option — **superannuation-level (60:40) exit** (16(11), 17(3)). Rule 18: dismissal/removal/compulsory retirement as penalty → corpus **NOT forfeited**, only premature (20:80) exit; same-PRAN continuation option; gratuity rules still apply (18(2)). Rule 19: pending departmental/judicial proceedings at retirement do **not** affect corpus — full 60:40 paid; only gratuity withheld (19(2)). Rule 20: death in service — OPS/EOP family pension if so opted; govt share to Government account, employee share lump sum to PFRDA nominee/legal heir; death attributable to service → CCS (EOP) Rules, 2023. Invalidation/disablement discharge also gets **death-gratuity slab rates** without the 5-year minimum (OM dt. 28.10.2022).

## M10 — Rules 21–30 (exit processing & residual)
Rule 21: HOD quarterly retirement list (1 Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct; to PAO by 31 Jan/30 Apr/31 Jul/31 Oct) for subscribers retiring in the next **12–15 months**; HOO promptly intimates non-superannuation exits to DDO/PAO (+ Directorate of Estates for allottees). Rule 22: HOO writes to Directorate of Estates ≥ **1 year** before retirement for NDC covering the period preceding **8 months** of retirement [VERIFY "8 months"]. Rule 23: claim to HOO **6 months before superannuation or on proceeding on LPR, whichever is EARLIER**; non-superannuation exits: immediately after orders; even online claims need a signed print-out to HOO; CRA generates Claim ID 6 months before superannuation. Rule 24: HOO→PAO (via DDO, Forms 4-A to 4-E) **4 months before** superannuation / **1 month after** other exits; PAO→CRA **1 month before** retirement; continuation/deferment option 15 days before superannuation in bare text (PFRDA Dec-2025 amendment removed the mandatory 15-day prior intimation; auto-continuation up to age 85). Rule 25: retirement on deputation to another Central dept — **borrowing** HOO; to State Govt/foreign service — **lending** HOO/cadre authority. Rule 26: Gazette notification (Gazetted) / office order (non-Gazetted) within **1 week**, copy to PAO. Rules 27–30: interpretation → MoPPGP; relaxation for undue hardship only with **MoPPGP concurrence**, reasons in writing (Rule 28); residuary matters per CCS (Pension) Rules/FR/SR/Govt orders if not repugnant (29(1)); repeal & saving (30).

## M11 — Forms
**Form 1** (Rule 10(1)): option OPS/EOP vs NPS on death/invalidation/disability — acknowledgement copy returned to servant. **Form 2** (Rule 10(3)): family details — original never substituted, changes in Col. 7 under HOO signature, all children/parents listed, judicially separated spouse included, fresh details at retirement. **Form 3** (Rule 16(7)): medical certificate (incapacity, less laborious fitness, "irregular or intemperate habits" clause, age by statement & appearance). **Forms 4-A to 4-E** (Rule 24(1)): HOO's covering letters to **PAO/AG through DDO** — 4-A: superannuation/VRS (Rule 12)/FR 56(k)/FR 56(j)/SVRS (all 60:40); 4-B: technical resignation/absorption; 4-C: resignation/penalty exits (20:80, 90-day rule); 4-D: invalidation/disablement; 4-E: death in service (incl. Rule 10(7) infructuous case).

### N-001 · Enacting formula and constitutional basis
**Rule/Form/OM:** Preamble, CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021 · **Topic:** Constitutional powers
**Q:** Under which exact constitutional provisions were the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021 framed, and whose consultation was mandatory?
**A:** Framed by the President under (1) the **proviso to Article 309** and (2) **clause (5) of Article 148**, **after consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India** in relation to persons serving in the Indian Audit & Accounts Department.
**Exam trap:** Do not answer "Article 309 only" or "PFRDA Act, 2013" — both the Art. 309 proviso and Art. 148(5) are invoked, with C&AG consultation for IA&AD.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules — G.S.R. 227(E) dt. 30.03.2021 (verified against Gazette text)
**Linked cards:** N-002, N-010

### N-002 · Notification header, short title & commencement
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 1(1)–(2); G.S.R. 227(E) · **Topic:** Timeline
**Q:** Give the short title, nodal department, notification number, date and commencement of the CCS (Implementation of NPS) Rules, 2021.

Notification — **G.S.R. 227(E), dated 30.03.2021**, Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i) [publication issue 31.03.2021 — VERIFY Gazette issue date]. ### N-003 · Rule 2 — who is covered
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 2 (opening words) · **Topic:** Application
**Q:** To which class of Government servants do the Rules apply?
**A:** Save as otherwise provided, they apply to **Government servants, including civilian Government servants in the Defence Services, appointed substantively to civil services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union, on or after 01.01.2004**.
**Exam trap:** (1) Civilian defence staff (incl. DAD) are covered; armed-forces personnel under the Army/Air Force/Navy Acts are not. (2) The cut-off is "on or after 01.01.2004", not the Rules' own commencement date.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules
**Linked cards:** N-004, N-009

### N-004 · Rule 2(a)–(h) — the eight statutory exclusions
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 2(a)–(h) · **Topic:** Exceptions
**Q:** List all eight categories excluded from the Rules.
**A:** The Rules do **not** apply to: **(a)** Railway servants; **(b)** persons in casual and daily-rated employment; **(c)** persons paid from contingencies; **(d)** members of the All India Services; **(e)** persons locally recruited for services in diplomatic, consular or other Indian establishments in foreign countries; **(f)** persons employed on contract; **(g)** persons whose terms of service are regulated by/under the Constitution or any other law in force; **(h)** persons to whom the CCS (Pension) Rules apply under any special/general Government order.
**Exam trap:** Probationers appointed substantively to regular posts **are** covered (Rule 6(6) — contribution during probation is mandatory). Contract/casual/contingency/Railway/AIS staff are out.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules
**Linked cards:** N-005, N-008, N-009, N-010, N-011

### N-005 · Rule 2(h) — OPS coverage by Government order (2020–2026)
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 2(h); DoPPW OM No. 57/05/2021-P&PW(B) dt. 03.03.2023; OM dt. 20.10.2023; OM dt. 17.02.2020; OM dt. 22.06.2026 [VERIFY] · **Topic:** Current-affairs update
**Q:** Which employees appointed on/after 01.01.2004 are excluded under Rule 2(h) because CCS (Pension) Rules apply to them by Government order?
**A:** (1) **Pre-22.12.2003 vacancy cases** — employees appointed against posts/vacancies **advertised/notified on or before 22.12.2003** (the date of the MoF NPS notification), including compassionate appointments applied for on/before 22.12.2003, were given a **one-time option** (OM dt. 03.03.2023) to opt for CCS (Pension) Rules — option to be exercised **by 31.08.2023**; appointing-authority decisions [VERIFY: OM text says by 31.10.2023; the draft said 30.11.2023]. Earlier OM dt. 17.02.2020 had required results declared before 01.01.2004; the 2023 OM relaxed it to advertisement/notification on or before **22.12.2003** — not 31.12.2003. (2) **Mobility/technical resignation cases** (OM dt. 20.10.2023): employees who joined Central/State Govt or an autonomous body before 01.01.2004 (or were covered under OPS via the 03.03.2023 OM) and moved to Central Govt with proper permission. (3) **Compassionate appointments** — DoPPW OM dt. 22.06.2026 clarified the governing date [VERIFY — check DoPT/DoPPW portal].
**Exam trap:** The crucial cut-off in the 03.03.2023 OM is **22.12.2003** (date of NPS notification), not 31.12.2003.

### N-006 · Autonomous bodies — no suo-moto application
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 2; DoPPW OM No. 57/03/2022-P&PW(B)/8361(8) dt. 25.10.2024; MoF (DoE) ID Note dt. 23.02.2026 [VERIFY] · **Topic:** Exception / current affairs
**Q:** Do the CCS (NPS) Rules, 2021 (and the Rule 10 OPS option OM dt. 26.10.2022) apply automatically to employees of Central Autonomous Bodies (CABs)?
**A:** **No.** Service matters of autonomous-body employees are not under DoPPW's jurisdiction; hence the CCS (NPS) Rules, 2021 and OM dt. 26.10.2022 are **not suo-moto applicable** to CAB employees (OM dt. 25.10.2024, verified). # Module 4 — Emoluments (Rule 5)

### N-026 · Rule 5(1) — what counts as "emoluments"
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 5(1); FR 9(21)(a)(i) · **Topic:** Formula
**Q:** What exactly constitutes "emoluments" for monthly NPS contributions?
**A:** Only **three** elements: (1) **Basic Pay** as defined in **FR 9(21)(a)(i)** (pay in the pay matrix for the post held substantively/officiating); (2) **NPA** for medical officers in lieu of private practice; (3) **admissible DA** for the calendar month. **Exam trap:** Contrast pension/gratuity rules: under CCS (Pension) Rules emoluments = pay + NPA (DA does not count for pension, but counts for gratuity). For **NPS** all three — pay + NPA + DA — count.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules
**Linked cards:** N-030, N-031

### N-027 · Rule 5(2)–(3) — leave and absence
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 5(2), 5(3); Proviso to Rule 7(1) · **Topic:** Proviso
**Q:** How are emoluments reckoned on paid leave, and on absence/EOL during part of a month?
**A:** Paid leave [5(2)]: pay + NPA + DA **actually drawn** as leave salary (e.g., HPL: half pay + admissible DA). Absence/EOL for whole or part of month [5(3)]: pay actually drawn for the duty/paid-leave part; **nil** if on EOL for the whole month. But if the leave is on **medical ground / civil commotion / useful higher studies**, the Government still contributes 14% on **notional full emoluments** (Proviso to Rule 7(1)) even where leave salary is nil or reduced.
**Exam trap:** Employee pays 10% only on actual drawn pay (nil on EOL); Government's notional 14% applies **only** to the three proviso grounds — private-affairs EOL gets ₹0 from Government (Rule 7(2)).

### N-028 · Rule 5(4) — suspension
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 5(4); Rule 6(2), 7(3) · **Topic:** Suspension
**Q:** What are "emoluments" during suspension?
**A:** The **subsistence allowance drawn** (plus DA admissible on it) — relevant only if the subscriber **opts to contribute** during suspension (Rule 6(2)); if he opts out, both shares are nil during suspension.
**Exam trap:** If the suspension period is finally treated as duty/paid leave, contributions are recalculated on full emoluments with PPF interest on the difference (provisos to 6(2)/7(3)); if it remains "not duty", no recalculation.

# Module 5 — Contributions, Interest & Investment (Rules 6–9)

---

### N-031 · Rules 6(1), 7(1), 7(5) — 10% / 14% and rounding
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 6(1), 7(1), 7(5) · **Topic:** Rates
**Q:** State the mandatory contribution percentages and the rounding rule.
**A:** Subscriber: **10%** of monthly emoluments (or as notified) — defined-contribution basis [6(1)]. Government: **14%** (raised from 10% w.e.f. **01.04.2019**) [7(1)]. Both are **rounded off to the next higher rupee** [6(1), 7(5)] — Rule 7(5) ensures the rounding also applies to notional-leave, subsistence and regularisation arrears. Government pays nothing when the subscriber is not required to contribute, subject to the Proviso to 7(1) [7(2)].
**Exam trap:** If a subscriber voluntarily contributes >10% under Rule 6(9), the Government share **stays at 14%** — no matching of the excess.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules
**Linked cards:** N-030, N-034, App. C

### N-032 · Rules 6(2), 7(3) — suspension
**Rule/Form/OM:** Rule 6(2); Rule 7(3) + 1st & 2nd provisos · **Topic:** Proviso
**Q:** State the complete position on contributions during suspension and after the inquiry.
**A:** During suspension the subscriber **may opt** to contribute 10% on subsistence allowance; if he does, Government contributes 14% on it [6(2), 7(3)]. **1st proviso to 7(3):** if he opts out, **no Government contribution** during suspension. If the suspension period is finally **treated as duty or paid leave**, both shares are recalculated on the entitled emoluments and the difference is credited **with PPF-rate interest** (provisos to 6(2)/7(3)); if it remains "not duty", no recalculation/refund.
**Exam trap:** Two traps — opt-out ⇒ Government also pays zero; exoneration ⇒ recalculation **with interest**, not just principal.
**Source/Status:** Bare Rules
**Linked cards:** N-028, N-036, App. E.7

### N-033 · Proviso to Rule 7(1) — the three exceptional leaves
**Rule/Form/OM:** Proviso to Rule 7(1); Rules 6(3), 7(2), 5(2) · **Topic:** Proviso
**Q:** When must the Government pay 14% on **notional full emoluments** despite nil/reduced leave salary?
**A:** When leave is granted (1) **on medical ground**, (2) due to **civil commotion** preventing joining/rejoining duty, or (3) for **higher studies useful in discharge of official duty** — and during such leave leave salary is **not payable (EOL)** or is payable **at less than full pay (e.g., HPL)**. Then Government pays 14% on full notional pay + NPA + DA; the subscriber pays 10% only on actual leave salary drawn (nil on EOL) [6(3), 5(2)]. Private/domestic EOL: employee ₹0, Government ₹0 [7(2)].
**Exam trap:** Medical HPL → employee 10% of half-pay drawn; Government 14% of **full** notional pay. Private EOL → both zero.

**Linked cards:** N-027, App. E.7

### N-061 · Rule 18 — dismissal / removal / compulsory retirement as penalty

**Q:** Is the NPS corpus forfeited on dismissal/removal/penal compulsory retirement?

### N-087 · UPS — switch back to NPS and forfeiture on penalty
**Rule/Form/OM:** CCS (UPS under NPS) Rules, 2025 (Rule 11); MoF notification Aug-2025; DoPPW OMs dt. 29.10.2025 (series of 10) · **Topic:** UPS switch

# Appendix G — Top 50 exam traps (consolidated)

1. Enacting power of CCS (NPS) Rules 2021 = **proviso to Art. 309 + Art. 148(5)** with C&AG consultation — not Art. 309 alone (N-001).
2. Rules commenced on **Gazette publication**, not retrospectively from 01.01.2004 (N-002).
3. Rule 2 exclusions are **eight** — (a) Railway, (b) casual/daily-rated, (c) contingency-paid, (d) AIS, (e) locally recruited abroad, (f) contract, (g) Constitution/other-law, (h) OPS-by-order (N-004).
4. Pre-2004 **temporary-status** casual labourers regularised later get **OPS with 50% service credit** (N-008).
5. CCS (NPS) Rules do **not** apply suo-moto to autonomous bodies (OM 25.10.2024, 8361(8)) (N-006).
6. Cut-off in OM 03.03.2023 is **22.12.2003**, not 31.12.2003; option by 31.08.2023 (N-005).
7. UPS is an **option under NPS**, not a repeal; UPS Rules = G.S.R. 599(E) dt. 02.09.2025, effective 01.04.2025 (N-086).
8. Accredited Bank may be **RBI itself**; money flows PAO/CDDO → Accredited Bank → Trustee Bank (N-012).
9. PFRDA Act sections: Authority 3(1), NPS 20, CRA 27, Pension Fund 27(3) (N-014).
10. "Foreign service" includes **domestic PSU deputation** — notional pay under Rule 5(6) (N-017, N-029).
11. HOD = Sch. I DFPR + President; HOO = Rule 14 DFPR + Competent Authority (N-018).
12. Registration chain = **3-3-3 working days**; PAO must send online **and** signed physical copy to CRA (N-022).
13. HOO pastes **certified** CSRF copy in Service Book within **5 working days** (N-023).
14. Rule 4(9): first contribution within 20 days OR last day of joining month — **whichever is LATER** (N-024).
15. Salary is **never stopped** for pending PRAN — pay after withholding 10%, later credited with PPF interest (N-024).
16. Emoluments = **Basic pay + NPA + DA only** — HRA/TA/special pay excluded (N-026).
17. Rounding of 10%/14% is **always to the next higher rupee** (N-031).
18. Government contribution raised 10% → **14% w.e.f. 01.04.2019** (N-031).
19. Suspension: opt-out ⇒ Government also pays **zero**; if finally treated as duty → recalculation **with PPF interest** (N-032).
20. Proviso to Rule 7(1): medical/civil-commotion/useful-study leave ⇒ Govt pays 14% on **full notional** even on EOL/HPL; private EOL ⇒ both zero (N-033).
21. Arrears count for the **month of payment**, not back-dated (N-034).
22. Monthly calendar: **20th** deduction → **25th** SCF → **last working day** remittance; **March → 1st working day of April** (N-035).
